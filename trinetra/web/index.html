Rules of engagement
  • Not hostile (birds, friendly drones): keep watching or ignore.
  • Hostile and more than 1 km away: alert the reaction team.
  • Hostile, inside 1 km and coming closer: soft kill. Hard kill only if the soft kill fails.
  • Hostile, inside 1 km but not coming closer: hand over to the next unit.
